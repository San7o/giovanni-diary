* The unknown giants

#+begin_quote
They say we stand
on the shoulders of giants

Yet how much of the world
runs on the shoulders of
the plumber
the electrician
the mailman
the farmer
the trucker
the builder
the soldier
the un-dreamed of.

The unknown giants
who built
who maintain
our home.
#+end_quote
