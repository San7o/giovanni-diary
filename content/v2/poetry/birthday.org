* Birthday

#+begin_quote
Vibrations reach my little ears,
lovely sounds amidst the tears.
Red-light lakes come falling here,
it's soft and warm, my comfy sphere.

I hear them speak of love and life,
of how to care through joy and strife.
I long to see it for myself—
I just may need a little help.

Time to stretch, I'm getting ready;
kicking hard, my movements steady.
I'll try again, and make my way,
could tomorrow be my birthday?
#+end_quote
