* Support

#+begin_quote
What am I scared of - you dare to ask
where are we going, battered in a mask
and having so much fear, anxiety steering,
how am I to handle such a feeling?

Maybe through support - I dare to say
shared at out best, not to astray
It's solidarity against swordplay.

But how do we know?
Why don't I feel aglow
yet I fight with my foe?

That - I confess - I do not understand
lost like shifting grains of sand
face to face with the very end.

Though it is a mystery,
and on struggle stands our history
the best I can do
is offer my support, to you.
#+end_quote
