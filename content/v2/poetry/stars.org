* The gaps between the stars

#+begin_quote
You may see me
on long nights,
when, defeated,
you raise your eyes.
I'm in the gaps
between
the stars,
where spoken words
remain untold,
just silent beats
we quietly hold.
It's lonely, lonely here.
It's cold, cold out here.
But if I look around,
past crumbs
of lonesome ground,
I may see you
on long nights,
when, defeated,
I raise my eyes.
In the gaps between
the stars.
#+end_quote
