* The gaps between the stars

#+begin_quote
You may see me
on the long nights,
when, defeated,
you raise your eyes.
I'm in the gaps
between
the stars,
where spoken words
remain untold,
just lonely beats
we quietly hold.
It's lonely, lonely here.
It's cold, really cold here.
But if I look around,
past crumbs
of lonesome ground,
I may see you
on the long nights,
when, defeated,
I raise my eyes.
In the gaps between
the stars.
#+end_quote
