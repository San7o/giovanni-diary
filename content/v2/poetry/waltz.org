* Waltz of minds alike

#+begin_quote
This is the waltz of minds alike.
It starts with eyes for our own kind,
a curious sight - it's rare to find.

Dance the waltz of threaded minds,
let our steps be truth's delight,
rotating magnets light up the night.

Play the waltz of shared insights,
same-phased waves reach greater heights,
harmonics flow like gentler tides.

Model truth through code and verse,
make sense of our universe.
Such is the waltz of minds alike.
#+end_quote
