* The slowest breath

#+begin_quote
The slowest breath may go unseen,
its movements subtle, in between.
Yet some perceive what others miss:
that every city, every road,
that every bridge, every stone,
even the glass of your phone,
breathes in the day, out the night,
breathes in the summer, out the winter,
breathes through your touch,
in silent cycles of expansion.
#+end_quote

11 November 2026
