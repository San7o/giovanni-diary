* Unpublished poems

Unfinished / failed poems.

** Belongings

#+begin_quote
Here, take all my belongings,
all my money, all my clothes.
For I am no servant
to these worldly goods.
I'll wear but rags,
that will be enough.
So I shall remain naked,
my mind clear and pure;
and hope to get a chance
to better hear the truth.
#+end_quote

** Round

#+begin_quote
Isn't it funny,
that if I travel far enough south,
past tamed hills and stormy seas,
I'll once again stand right here?
#+end_quote
