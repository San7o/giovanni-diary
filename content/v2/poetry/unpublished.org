** Dam

#+begin_quote
Once upon a time,
rivers of clean water
came flowing down
the valley of time.

Then there was a Dam,
an human construction,
blocking the stream,
trapping the water.

And the water began
piling up and up,
millions of liters
submerged the valley.

Until it could hold
no more.

So the Dam was opened;
with force, the crying
water went out, years
of repressed flow.

Now free to escape,
pouring out like blood,
water thus became
and energy source.
#+end_quote

** Belongings

#+begin_quote
Here, take all my belongings,
all my money, all my clothes.
For I am no servant
to these worldly goods.
I'll wear but rags,
that will be enough.
So I shall remain naked,
my mind clear and pure;
and hope to get a chance
to better hear the truth.
#+end_quote

** Round

#+begin_quote
Isn't it funny,
that if I proceed far enough south
down by tamed hills and stormy seas
I'll once again stand right here?
#+end_quote
