* My dear elitist

#+begin_quote
It must feel good, my dear elitist,
to be right about everything.
Up in your tower of grand ideologies
and paper degrees in superior studies.
And only your wars are deemed to be "just",
suffering ever happens to the ones of your kind.

You'd be quick to point out that I am different,
a dangerous species, to deserve anger and fear.
For I opened my mind, to listen, to think,
You can be better than this, you can, my dear.
#+end_quote

08 November 2026
