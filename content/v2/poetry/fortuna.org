* Fortuna, I fear you not

#+begin_quote
I fear you not, Fortuna, the foreverblind
Unjust! I cry - trapped in this grind
I'm tarnished, sobbing; I'm being played
Yet hellbent I stand: I march straight.

Spin your wheel! Spin it once more!
dictate the rules - unmighty whore
I take it all, I'll feel it whole
if such is destiny, be it my core
#+end_quote
