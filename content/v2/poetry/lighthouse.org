* Lighthouse

#+begin_quote
Howling winds came down to me,
with blows that stir
the restless sea.

They've buried the sky,
hid maps from my sight;
there's no land to dock,
stranded in a boat.

Still, a star lights up from afar:
it's a lighthouse, blooming for us.
Its keeper will guide me to shore.
Hope returns where none was before.

May I reflect this very light,
through every act of mine.
Like the moon that, in the night,
still shines through darkest times.
#+end_quote

03 November 2026
