* The Dam within

#+begin_quote
Once upon a time,
rivers of clear water
came flowing down
the valley of time.

Then rose a dam,
a human construction,
blocking the stream,
trapping the water.

And the water began
to gather and rise;
millions of liters
buried the valley.

Until it could hold
no more.

Then the dam broke open;
with force, the crying
waters burst forth,
years of repressed flow.

Now free to escape,
pouring out like blood,
the waters turn their force
to power born anew.
#+end_quote

18 November 2026
