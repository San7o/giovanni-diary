* Shoes

#+begin_quote
I've worn these shoes
for quite some time.
Their soles grew flat,
their shape undone.

I'll hang them on the wall
to see them one more time.
The company of a friend,
a ground for me to stand.

They carry traces of the years,
of quiet joys and hidden fears.
Of all the times we stood our ground,
and all the wonders we have found.

The stones of city streets,
worn down by countless feet.
The trains that carried us
through lands both near and far.

The journeys in the snow
through hidden paths below.
The nights we spent in huts,
where silence held us close.

The floors where I have played,
and learned the steps of dance.
The pavement of my home,
where I know I belong.

They saw it all,
every step, every fall.
Through warmth and bitter cold,
I lived, I felt it all.
#+end_quote

18 November 2026
