* Empty Lonely hills

#+begin_quote
Longing on my mind
looking for a sign
on this bench, between two worlds
one my home, and one my soul.

On the left the sea
shouting: "call to me!"
Loud in summer time
of dances, sun and wine

But my eyes look west
dreaming all the rest

Empty lonely hills
Oh, empty lonely hills
#+end_quote
