* Patterns

#+begin_quote
I can't help but link the dots — don't you really see?
there's a pattern within nature: all around you and me.
Let me model all of it! Let me find relationships!
As a system of equations, better with computer chips!
Help me now discover beauty, let the implications follow;
all I want is to understand, and to predict our sorrows.
Thereby explain the earth's motion, and of the seasons—
But to know all's origins, that I fail to reason.

Yet, to formalize the infinite,
isn't that truly intimate?
#+end_quote
