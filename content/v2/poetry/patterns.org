* Patterns

#+begin_quote
I can't help but notice — don't you agree?
there's a pattern in nature, It's among you and me.
Let me model all of it! Let me find relationships!
With rules and axioms, and computer chips!
I'll define this beauty, demonstration follows,
so I shall understand, and predict our sorrows.
Thereby explain the motion of the seasons,
thou to know all's origin, that I fail to reason.
#+end_quote

17 October 2026
