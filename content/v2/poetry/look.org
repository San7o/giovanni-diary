* Where few eyes look

#+begin_quote
As I watch my weary steps,
at the ground that lies ahead,
I turn my head, leave the path,
towards lands and skies afar.

And I see what was unseen,
find the beauty lying between.
As distant lenses gather light,
I find new worlds within my sight.

How unlikely that I should find it
on a lonely little dot.
So dear to me, so dear —
beauty lies where few eyes look.
#+end_quote
