* The stage

#+begin_quote
Fingers dance as clockwork gears,
muscle memory shakes out the fear.
Hands, like tides, swing in rhyme,
we've learned all steps; we'll be fine.
Arms, like currents, stream behind.
Lungs, don't fail me, please be kind.
I've practiced this ballet countless times,
each movement falls just in line.
I am prepared — take me to the stage.
#+end_quote
