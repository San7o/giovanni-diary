* 'Tis in truth

#+begin_quote
'Tis in this truth, indeed—
that we are taught through hardship
to feel, in darkness aplenty,
our birthed humanity—
where our soul still stands.

Scarred by this run,
our skin made hollow,
kindling one bonfire, then the next,
till the last ember fades—

and still,
fate calls us forth
to tread the road again.
#+end_quote
