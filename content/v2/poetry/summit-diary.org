* Summit diary

#+begin_quote
Up I go, one step, once more;
paths were worn here long before
by all brave souls who climb this stone,
to reach the top, to breathe, restore.

Down by the cross awaits us all
a precious trace, so mild, so small;
right where the words survive the fall:
The Summit Diary - we like to call.

And as we hikers come and go,
while nature runs her endless course,
fragments of souls remain enclosed,
beyond the reach of time's harsh force.

Within these leaves are all our sounds,
our smiles, our sweat from all around.
Our deepest grief, our greatest hope,
a self portrait of our shared globe.

But when it's time to leave the summit,
to reach my home right down the valley,
there'll be my name marked in this diary,
and blank space for the next to find it.
#+end_quote

31 October 2026
