* Minerva

#+begin_quote
I still await that distant day
you rest your gaze upon the ground.
There'll be a kind man there to stay,
who hopes his worth may yet be found.

He's calling up, his voice so faint,
coughing out shy words from far away:
"Teach me to think, to see, to dare;
perhaps one day you'll find me there."

He longs to learn the ways you know,
and let your wisdom in him grow.
He strives to reach your lofty height,
and hopes one day to catch your sight.

Though they exchanged but a few lines,
and briefly caught each other's eyes,
what he is struggling to verbalize
is how precious was that fleeting time.
#+end_quote
