* Minerva

#+begin_quote
I just look forward to the very day
you rest your gaze upon the ground.
There'll be a kind man there to stay,
who hopes his worth may yet be found.
He's calling up, unsure what to say,
coughing out shy words from far away:
"I wish you well! Accept my prayer!",
for he truly admires the way you are.
Though they met for but some lines,
and briefly caught each other's eyes,
what he is struggling to verbalize
is how precious was that fleeting time.
#+end_quote
