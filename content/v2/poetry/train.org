* Train

#+begin_quote
Squeezed in this old, familiar train,
me and my three bags — that's all I claim.
But please, please take this seat,
for we are alike; I sense your need.

I'll hold on and wait — so brief is time —
until I get off, this short trip of mine.
For we are all the same, standing up in pain,
trying our best, even when in vain.
#+end_quote

16 August 2026
