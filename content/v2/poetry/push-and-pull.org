* Push and pull

#+begin_quote
Now I am drowning
in deep blue waters;
I gasp for air,
my head aches.

Then I am dancing
amidst the crowd;
breathing in deeply
beneath clear blue skies.

Now I am falling,
crashing to the ground.
I am not in control —
how disheartening.

All the while, I carry on,
through every push and pull.
It hurts, it really does —
yet that's how I grow.
#+end_quote
