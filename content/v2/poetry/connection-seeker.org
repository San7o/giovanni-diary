* The connection seeker

#+begin_quote
The connection seeker is a curious creature,
he wanders and wonders to note every feature,
for truth and its threads are his lofty teacher.

As he spots a pattern, he lights up a flame,
revealing the paths that no one could name,
paving the way for seekers to aim.

Then he links the ember to thoughts he can trace,
to a web of discoveries with no single face,
painting a picture of his home, of our place.

Day after day he cares much for this mesh,
as he holds it dear in his mind and his flesh,
for at times he feels part of this whole.
#+end_quote
