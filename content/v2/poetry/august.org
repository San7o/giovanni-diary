* Busy week of August

#+begin_quote
Squeezed, in this old familiar train,
me and my three bags - that is all I claim.
But please, please take this seat
as we are alike, as I feel your need.

I'll hold and wait - so brief is time -
to get off this train - this short trip of mine.
As we are all the same, standing up in pain
trying our best, even when in vain

in a busy week of August.
#+end_quote

16 August 2026
