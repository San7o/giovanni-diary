* Foot notes

#+begin_quote
Reading pages from my diaries,
dusting photos, and the memories;
making sure I miss no thing,
no detail left between.

And I will write an entry for each,
in the diary of my mistakes:
of what went wrong, and why it did,
so I may learn and not repeat —

I left some footnotes, too,
of lessons learned as I grew;
they became poems.
#+end_quote

20 November 2026
