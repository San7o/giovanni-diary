Hi, I am Giovanni.

You'll see I am a particularly curious person, but quite reserved and
introspective (INFJ, Enneagram 5). You'll find me in libraries and quiet places.
I enjoy low-level [[https://github.com/San7o/][programming]], journaling,
writing short stories and poetry, reading non-fiction, and playing the piano (my
favourite composers are Ludovico Einaudi and "Sleeping at Last"). I also
participate in local communities through volunteering for spreading culture,
history and helping less fortunate people, and I used to dance folk music!
