Hi, I am Giovanni

I am a particularly curious and kind person, but quite reserved and
introspective (INTJ, Enneagram 5w4). You'll find me always studying in libraries
and silently reading or reflecting in quiet isolated places. I often understand
both sides of an argument, and I am a better listener than I am a speaker.

I have been journaling regularly for more than 4 years, in different forms over
time (both physically and digitally, privately and publicly). This is part of my
life, it helps me clear my mind, understand myself and the world around me. I
also enjoy writing poetry and short stories for the same reasons, although I am
quite new to this form of writing, and playing the piano.

My favorite piano composer is Ludovico Einaudi, and my overall favorite
songwriter / composer is "Sleeping at Last" (I love "Saturn" and his Enneagram
songs, and how he structures his albums). When I have time I read non-fiction,
usually related to the history and evolution of technology which I find really
interesting (especially in the last century, Bell Labs, Unix, ARPANET...).

I have had the fortune to live some years close to the mountains, I discovered I
really like hiking (not extreme ones) and generally spending time in nature
(I dream of living in a small hut in close contact with nature). I also used
to volunteer in some local associations spreading culture, history, outdoor
activities and helping less fortunate people, and I used to dance folk music and
play in theaters.

Professionally I am studying to become a software engineer, I am focusing on low
level programming like operating systems, firmware / embedded development,
datacenter infrastructure and administration. I am also interested in
networking, cybersecurity and High Performance Computing. Here is where I
spend most of my time.

If you have matching interests with me, or a compatible personality, feel free
to write to me! I am a friendly person, although I may take time to reply.
