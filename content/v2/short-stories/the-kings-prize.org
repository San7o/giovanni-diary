* The King's prize

** Preamble

A powerful King, known for his great wealth and power, ruled over a kingdom
stretching as far as the eye could see. To celebrate his coming birthday, out of
might or maliciousness, he summoned the people of his kingdom for a great
announcement, nobody excluded. The following day, right after all guests were
received and welcomed with fresh food and warm drinks, he addressed the group:

"On such a magnificent day, I've prepared a gift to you all: I will give you
this land right under you feet, to do as you please! Furthermore, a great
treasure is hidden for you to find: whoever discovers it shall keep all the
prize!".

And what a memorable day that was. Music and fireworks could be heard from all
over the kingdom; the young and the old both drinking and dancing, what a good
and generous king they had, they all thought. But soon, the fests turned quiet
as the one thing kept their mind at unrest: "Where could the treasure be?".

Nobody really knew; distant news of someone already looking for it was spreading
like a virus: "What if somebody were to find the treasure before me? He would
keep it all to himself!", most thought silently. Thus the quest for or the
great treasure began.
