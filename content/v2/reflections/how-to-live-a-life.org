* How to live a life

Be respectful, be polite, be reasonable, be supportive, be presentable, be
diligent, be grateful, be humble, be kind, speak clearly, seek truth and
sincerity, care about others, eat healthy, exercise, sleep 8 hours, nurture
your curiosity, reflect on yourself and the world, be a writer, be grounded in
reality, be a scientist, be optimistic, try your best, lead by example, take
initiative. Don't you dare go hollow.
