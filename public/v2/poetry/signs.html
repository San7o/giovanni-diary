Signs

I've hiked far from the place I was born, crossed seas and hills, endured the storms, looking back at the branches I have taken, I keep wondering if I was mistaken.

If I had a map I would see all ahead, to plan the road and meet you there. If I had some wings I would fly to the end, my legs and back have been overwhelmed.

But all I have is a compass and a bag, and hundreds of notes from the peers I met. And sometimes I see, marked on the trees, signs of relief: I'm on the right track.
