The connection seeker

The connection seeker is a curious creature, he wanders and wonders to note every feature, for truth and its threads are his lofty teacher.

As he spots a pattern, he lights up a flame, revealing the paths that no one could name, paving the way for seekers to aim.

Then he links the ember to thoughts he can trace, to a web of discoveries with no single face, painting a picture of his home, of our place.

Day after day he cares for his mesh, he holds it dear in his mind and his flesh. Forever curious, never at rest.
