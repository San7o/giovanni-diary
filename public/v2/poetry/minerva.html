Minerva

I still await that distant day you rest your gaze upon the ground. A kind man waits to cross your way, and hopes his worth may yet be found.

He calls to you, his voice so faint, with humble words from far away: "Teach me to think, to see, to dare; perhaps one day you'll find me there."

He longs to learn the ways you know, and let your wisdom in him grow. He strives to reach your lofty height, and hopes one day to catch your sight.

Though they exchanged a few short lines, they briefly met each other's eyes. No words could ever quite convey how precious was that fleeting day.
