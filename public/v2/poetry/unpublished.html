Belongings

Here, take all my belongings, all my money, all my clothes. For I am no servant to these worldly goods. I'll wear but rags, that will be enough. So I shall remain naked, my mind clear and pure; and hope to get a chance to better hear the truth.

Round

Isn't it funny, that if I travel far enough south, past tamed hills and stormy seas, I'll once again stand right here?
