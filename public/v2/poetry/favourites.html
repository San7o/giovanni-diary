To the unknown journeyman

To the unknown journeyman I promise to show courage in upholding my vows

What is an adventure if not the commitment to keep going?

By Protesilaos Stavrou, 2025-08-11, original link.

Truly beautiful and powerful words from Prot, as always. I feel greatly moved by this poem.

From Wikipedia, a journeyman is "a worker, skilled in a given building trade or craft, who has successfully completed an official apprenticeship qualification.". He is someone that showed dedication and fought through adversities to master his skill: someone can can be looked up to. Yet there are many "unknown" journeymen, especially in the world of programming, there are so many professionals that are doing excellent and critical work but are greatly under-recognized and underappreciated (maybe Prod is thinking of his condition, and the one of other maintainers).

Someone that mastered their skills can motivate us into working harder and harder. It surely does for me. He made a promise to himself, and so do I, to never give up.

The second part of the poem explores this idea, and it words it beautifully.

I find a deep stoic meaning in this poem, to keep going through life nevertheless.

Behind the glass

They speak in mirrors, polished clean, where every fracture looks like light.

Carefully arranged reflections to prove they can see themselves.

They call it understanding,
the same way one may memorize a new language, yet never reshape their tongue to speak it.

So when they speak, the words can be heard,
yet find no true translation.

Blue Flower

Blue flower — wonderous flower, You’ll wonder no more. Distinguished from the field; Still the field is power.

Be thee a hole in Earth, for: Weather comes like the ocean. Delicate flower, grounded by rhythms; Your weary soul drags its flesh to bed.

Lucky flower — uncursed flower, Parted your lips and was heard; And lashing had fun, you never Knew better, blue flower.

I really like the repetition of flower in the first sentence.

Old home

I long… I long for my old home, A cherished memory, now I am alone— Alone within this dark forest, Where none may ever rest.

I cannot sleep, I cannot dream; I linger here, or so it seems. The hours pass, yet never cease— And dawn never breaks to grant me peace.

This wood is home to monsters old, Who seek a treasure more than gold; Warmth they seek so much— For cold is life without human touch.

And truly, better it is to burn, Than to be alone and ever mourn.
