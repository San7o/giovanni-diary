Town

Challenge: You win the lottery and receive a ticket of an all-expenses-covered vacation to a town you have never heard of. Upon reaching the town, you find out there only 6 more people in the town and all of them has reached there through a lottery ticket- just like you. Write about how you connected with those strangers and if you were able to find your way back safely. Also, include auditory and olfactory imagery to describe the town's uniqueness.

—

  • Figure out how does the city look like and how to describe it
  • Figure out the characters
  • Figure out a plot
  • Figure out an ending
